# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want claude-jam to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their experience,
background, identity or platform.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming a mistake was careless.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and PRs, and may ban anyone who breaks these rules.
To report a problem, use the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/roypadina/claude-jam/security/advisories/new)
form (it reaches the maintainer only), or open a Discussion asking for a private channel. Do not post
details of the incident publicly.
